# Sales-Analysis-Project
**Excel sales Analysis project**- Analyzed Rs 15.5L sales data using pivot tables and Pivot charts to identify revenue trends, highest/lowest sales dates, and the impact of product-mix on revenue.
### Project overview
Analyzed **6 days of sales data** with Total **RS 15.5L** from **55 units** to understand daily revenue trends and identify the factors driving sales performance.

### Keys insights
- **HIGHEST SALES**: 04/07/2026 - RS 3,40,000
- **LOWEST SALES**: 02/07/2026 - RS 2,00,000
- **KEY OBSERVATION**: The same **11 UNITS** generated RS 2.5L revenue on 3rd July 2026 and RS 3.4L on 4th July 2026.
- **KEY FINDING**: The RS 90,000 revenue difference was driven by **product mix**.
      - Laptop sales increased from **1 unit to 4 units** (RS 50K per unit).
      - Mobiles sales decreased from **10 units to 7 units** (RS 20K per unit).

This shows that **the number of units sold alone does not determine revenue; product mix also has a significant impact on total revenue.**
